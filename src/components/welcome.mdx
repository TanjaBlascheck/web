# Welcome to EuroVis 2027 in Stuttgart

The exciting and vibrant field of visualization is an important research area due to the increasing popularity of data-driven decision-making and its wide range of applications. In general, our ability to collect, store, and archive data vastly exceeds our ability to derive useful knowledge and insight from it. This is a ubiquitous problem. Data visualization is key to understanding large, complex data sets and processes by exploiting the human visual system. Data visualization leverages computer graphics, AI, machine learning, and perception to provide a visual overview, explore, analyze, and present phenomena that are often difficult to understand.

The objective of EuroVis is to foster exchange between visualization researchers and practitioners and to attract researchers and industry partners from around the globe to enter this rapidly growing area of research. EuroVis has expanded its scope to encompass all areas of visualization and visual analytics.

EuroVis has been an international Eurographics visualization event held in Europe annually since 1990. It began as the Eurographics Workshop on Visualization in Scientific Computing (ViSC) and became the joint Eurographics/IEEE Visualization Symposium (VisSym) in 1999. It was renamed to EuroVis in 2005 and became a Eurographics Conference in 2012. Since 2008, its full-paper proceedings have been published as a special issue of Computer Graphics Forum (EG/Wiley CGF).
